Creative Octopus: Agile Coordination Lab

This app treats the octopus as a metaphor for creative educational action. Each arm represents a different technique, strategy, wisdom tradition, skill, medium, or way of knowing.

The octopus is most creative when it has freedom to explore, enough shared orientation to coordinate its limbs, and enough situational wisdom to choose fitting moves. It becomes least creative when it is stiff, over-controlled, and only obeys orders.

Reading the octopus

Free arms: autonomy lets techniques, skills and media explore the situation rather than merely execute instructions.
Wise coordination: creativity improves when exploratory moves are compared, combined and reflected on.
Rigid orders: high constraint with low autonomy produces stiffness, imitation and brittle compliance.
