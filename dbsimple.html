Noise vs Vocabulary vs Constraint (and why it oscillates)

This demo models a "teacher register" moving along a coupled track: low vocab + low noise (expert/controlled code) ↔ high vocab + high noise (legal/discursive code). As noise increases, the teacher adds constraint/redundancy (definitions, exceptions, cross-refs). Reducing vocabulary reduces effective noise (fewer distinct symbols → fewer meaningful corruption outcomes).

1) Channel: noise ↑ → constraint ↑

2) Oscillation: teacher chases two clusters
