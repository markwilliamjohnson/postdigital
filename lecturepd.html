Lecture → Discussion: constrained teacher, selective engagement, put-off narrowing, balkanisation

Teacher delivers a lecture under movement constraints. Students are distributions with increasing SD to the right. After the lecture, only a small subset (those with some overlap + willingness) engage in discussion. Institutional facilitation increases reach and engagement. Some students become put-off: they move away to the right and narrow their discourse (lower SD), forming right-side clusters that produce balkanisation.
