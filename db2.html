Double-Blind Pedagogy: Oscillation Without Convergence

The teacher oscillates between a restricted technical discourse (digitalisation agenda) and a nuanced learner discourse. The two domains drift apart over time. The teacher never converges: overlap is temporary and structurally unstable.
This models a *double-bind*: moving toward learners breaks alignment with digitalisation; moving toward digitalisation breaks mapping to learner meaning. The system cannot stabilise.
