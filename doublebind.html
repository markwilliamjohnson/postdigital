Teacher Oscillation: Restricted Agenda ↔ Nuanced Subject ↔ Students

Teacher oscillates because restricted agenda language (low σ) can’t map onto nuanced subject discourse (higher σ). Moving right requires nuance (σ↑), but that reduces overlap with the agenda — pulling the teacher back left.
